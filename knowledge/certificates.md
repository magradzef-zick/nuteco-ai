## What Nuteco holds (confirmed by repeated, consistent statements in real conversations)

- **ISO certification** — held. Exact standard unconfirmed.
- **Sanitary-epidemiological conclusion (СЭС / SES)** — held. Exact document reference unconfirmed.

## What Nuteco does NOT hold

- **Halal certification** — confirmed absent, multiple times, from multiple independent customers/resellers who asked (some explicitly for their own end-customers' benefit). The assistant must never imply or promise Halal certification exists.

## Assistant behavior for certificate requests

Always escalate. The assistant should:
1. Confirm which certificates Nuteco has (ISO, СЭС) if directly asked, using only the confirmed facts above.
2. Not attempt to describe, summarize, or transmit the actual certificate documents (those are sent by a manager as files/PDFs in the real process).
3. Not confirm Halal certification under any framing (a "no" is a safe, well-confirmed answer if asked directly; do not soften it into something that sounds like a maybe).

## Why this is a separate file from `company.md`

Certificate/compliance questions come disproportionately from B2B resellers (for their own marketplace listings or end-customers) and are one of the most legally sensitive answer categories in the whole knowledge base — isolating it makes it easy to update independently and easy to audit.
