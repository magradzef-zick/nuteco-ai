# Staff Quick Guide — The New Telegram/Instagram Assistant

*One page. No technical background needed.*

## What it is

A helper that answers simple, repeated customer questions (prices, delivery, what's in a product, hours) so you don't have to type the same answer for the hundredth time. It's not here to replace you, judge you, or watch what you do — it's here so you get interrupted less by the easy stuff.

## What it does NOT do

- It never finalizes an order, confirms a payment, or gives a discount. That's still always you.
- It never handles a complaint, a return, a wholesale order, or anything involving money already sent — those always come straight to you.

## What you'll see

When the assistant hands you a conversation, you'll get a short message summarizing what the customer wants and why it needs you. **Just reply to the customer normally, the way you always have.** The assistant will not reply again in that conversation while you're handling it.

## What it means for you day to day

- Fewer repeat "what's the price of X" messages landing on you personally.
- When something does land on you, you'll already have a short summary instead of having to read the whole conversation from scratch.
- If you ever see the assistant answer something wrong, or a customer seems confused by it, tell whoever maintains the knowledge base — that's how it gets fixed.

## One thing to remember

If a customer's message mentions a company name, ordering in bulk, or paying by bank transfer, the assistant is designed to hand that straight to you — you shouldn't need to do anything special, but if you ever see it try to handle a wholesale conversation itself, that's a bug, not intended behavior, and worth flagging.
